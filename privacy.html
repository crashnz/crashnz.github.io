Privacy Policy

We collect personal information from you, including information about your: name contact information location computer or network interactions with us Details about your dog.

We collect your personal information in order to: Help identify our customers and their dogs. Identify the approximately location of where our customers live, so we can identify areas for marketing. Provide a means of contacting our customers in regards to bookings.

Besides our staff, we share this information with: local pet clinics and pet stores in order to build relationships and offer our clients special deals.

Providing some information is optional. If you choose not to enter your name, contact details or information about your dog, we are likely to refuse your booking. We keep your information safe by encrypting all communications and store the data encrypted in the database which can only be accessed by authorised personal.

We keep your information for three years after the last point of contact at which point we anonymising your identifying personal details. You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong. If you’d like to ask for a copy of your information, or to have it corrected, please contact us at admin@snazzypaws.co.nz.
